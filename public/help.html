Help

Features

  • New inspirational Bible verse daily
  • Beautiful background images matching verse themes
  • Light & Dark mode support (use T or toggle)
  • Keyboard shortcuts for easy navigation (see below)
  • Share verses with others (use S or button)
  • Installable for an app-like experience (see below)

Keyboard Shortcuts

Space
Get a new verse
T
Toggle dark/light theme
S
Share current verse (uses native share if available, otherwise copies to clipboard)
? or H
Show this help page
Esc
Return to verses page

Installation (Offline Access)

Add Daily Verse to your Home Screen for quick access and an app-like experience, even when offline.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Daily Verse in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Choose a name (or keep the default) and tap "Add"

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Daily Verse in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top right
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  4. Choose a name (or keep the default) and tap "Add"

Troubleshooting

  • If you encounter any issues, please check your internet connection and try again later.
  • If the app is not loading properly, try clearing your browser cache and restarting the app.
