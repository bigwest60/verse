Add to Home Screen

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Daily Verse in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Choose a name (or keep the default) and tap "Add"

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Daily Verse in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top right
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  4. Choose a name (or keep the default) and tap "Add"

Features

  • Beautiful backgrounds that change with each verse theme
  • Dark and light mode support
  • Works offline once installed
  • Share verses with friends
  • Themed verses for different topics (love, guidance, strength, etc.)
